<h2> What does Funding Cover? </h2>

 * **Stipend** Tax-free living allowance, paid monthly or quaterly 
 * **Tuition Fees** ~£5,000.00 covered for UK or home students 
 * **Training Support Fund** Variable funding for conferences, training, and equipment

<h2> How much? </h2>

Minimum stipend figures for 2026/27 compared to median UK salary after tax (£25,271)

* **Standard (Outside London)** £21,805
* **London Weighting** £23,805

_The TechExpert Pilot offers an additional £10,000 to UK home students at select CDTs across the country including StatML, STOR-i and AGQ. read more at: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/techexpert_

<h2> Important Figures to Know  for International Students</h2> 

UKRI rules cap international places at 30% per cohort for CDTs. This inlcudes EU and EEA students. 
 
 <h3> The Fee Gap Problem </h3>

UKRI funding only covers fees at the UK home rate (~£5,00 per year). International students are charged overseas fees between £20,000-£30,000 per year.

This difference is called the fee gap.

* Some CDTs/departments cover the gap entirely, these are often advertised separately as “fully funded PhD for international students”

* Others require you to find a separate scholarship to cover the gap. Some countries have local schemes to help with this, or companies may offer to sponsor your PhD if it is industry focused.

> Always ask explicitly: “Does this studentship cover international fees in full”
