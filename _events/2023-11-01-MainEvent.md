## About
You can find the PhD Your Way 2023 Information Booklet here: [PhD Your Way 2023 Info Book](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZwbijcVAhfOTbRsdphSB-a-qoWK3yOFe/view?usp=share_link)

PhD Your Way 2023 will take place via Zoom from 3pm to 5.30pm on Wednesday 1st November. There'll be talks, panel discussions and the opportunity to talk with current maths PhD students. 

It can be a struggle to find clear information on PhDs, especially for people without current connections to academia. For PhD Your Way 2023, we will provide a "blank slate" perspective on applying, starting from:

- what exactly does a maths PhD student do?
- what are the experiences of underrepresented groups?
- how do PhD applications work? 
- what should you consider before applying?

This should help you make an informed decision on applying for mathematics PhDs in the UK. You'll also have the opportunity to talk in an informal context, like a job-fair, with maths PhD students from over 20 different UK universities
