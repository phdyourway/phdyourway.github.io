This webinar will begin with short presentations from our panellists, followed by an open Q&A where attendees can put their own questions to the panel.

Our speakers will share their own experiences of requesting and arranging reasonable adjustments during a Maths PhD to accommodate their needs, reflect on the process of doing so within their department, and what they wish supervisors and departments understood better. This session is aimed at prospective and current students with additional needs who want practical insight into disclosure and adjustments in a PhD workplace.
